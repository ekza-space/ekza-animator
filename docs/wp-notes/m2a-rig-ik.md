## Interpretations and decisions

| # | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| M2A-A1-1 | **Knee hinge from the lower leg** (deviation of §5.2.1): legs use `x̂⁰` = the rest direction of the lower leg (`normalize(x̂⁰_L × (0, 0, −1))`), arms keep the upper arm. Straight legs give the same hinge; the R07 table still applies when the cross product is short. | Polybot's thighs splay 28.6° outward over vertical shins. The upper-bone formula tilted Polybot's knee hinge by 28.6° in the frontal plane, so knee flexion about x — the FK controls' and the normalized humanoid convention — was no hinge motion: E2E-M2A-06's clip kept Polybot's left leg FK at 11.0° (spec: IK). With the lower leg it imports as IK (0.05°, 0.003 mm). The spec's own rationale ("the shin swings back") names the lower bone. |
| M2A-A1-2 | **Match pole rule relative to the IK's own mid joint** (refinement of §5.6): β is the spec's `smoothstep(2°, 10°, θ)·smoothstep(0, 0.5, m̂·h)`, but the blend turns `h` about `x̂` by `β·ψ`, ψ the signed angle from `R_x̂(ω)·h` to the FK offset `m`, where ω is the direction (about `x̂`, from the hinge-implied direction) of the mid-joint offset the IK produces: computed from the rest geometry for a chain bent by `t` (`TwoBoneCore.offsetAngleAt`), with `t` = the FK pose's own hinge bend (the swing-twist hinge part of the mid bone's rotation relative to the upper bone, against the rest; on the flexion side) for IK bends up to 10°, and the IK's bend for the FK reach from 20° (smoothstep between). The pole sits at `b + p̂·D − (m − (m·p̂)p̂)`, so its offset from the upper–end line points along `p̂` (the op's twist aligns the hinge-implied direction with that offset). | `normalize((1 − β)h + βm̂)` assumes `m̂ = h` for every hinge pose, which holds only when the rest bend lies exactly in the hinge plane. Seed-san's knees are 0.7° off it (the literal rule left 0.22° / 0.06 mm on hinge poses; gate 1e-6), Polybot's splayed legs and arms far off it (ω runs from 90° at straight to 20° at 30° of bend on Polybot's arms). Every hinge pose gives ψ = 0 (the exact M1-style pole; ≤ 4e-14 on both fixtures); from 20° of bend an off-hinge knee at β = 1 gets the IK knee exactly into the FK bend plane; near-straight and hyperextended limbs (β = 0) use `h`. The FK hinge bend is a smooth function of the FK rotations; the IK's bend for a reach changes like 1/sin t near straight (the reach singularity), which is why it enters only from 10°. ψ cannot wrap while β > 0 (`|ω| < π/2` on the flexion side and `m̂·h > 0`). First version (`6eae6a9`…`9955177`): ψ from an extra IK evaluation — discontinuous (fix round 1). |
| M2A-A1-3 | **Amendment B always, plus a continuous amendment C** (`ik/core.ts`): the op's twist always aligns the hinge-implied direction `x̂ × k` with the pole (the spec does it only for `|m| ≤ 1e-3·L`; `m` is used when `x̂ ∥ k`). Amendment C: measure the bend by `t`, the angle about the hinge from the bend of greatest reach (a reach has the two solutions `t = ±s`), and let `T ≥ 0` be the rest's hyperextension (`t⁰ = −T`). M1 takes `t = s`; with `T > 0` the solver takes `t = −s` for `s ≤ T` (targets between the rest reach `d⁰` and full reach: the hyperextended branch, exact, the rest itself at `s = T`), `t = s − 2T·(1 − y)²` with `y = (s − T)/W`, `W = min(2T, π − T)` inside the band `T < s < T + W`, and `t = s` beyond (the flexion branch, exact). Matching (`bandShift` in `limb/match.ts`) places the IK control on the reach ray at the distance for which the solver takes the FK bend (`distanceForBend`): inside the hand while the FK bend lies in the band, at the hand otherwise. | For rests bent about their hinge both B choices agree; rests that bend off the hinge (Polybot) would swing the knee sideways as the leg flexes if `m` were aligned with the pole. For C, the spec's rest exactness (1e-7 on hyperextended rests: Seed-san's elbows −1.38°, Polybot's −4.87°) and "IK bends only in flexion" cannot both hold with a continuous solution: the rest bend `−T` lies on the hyperextended side, every target closer than `d⁰` is reached exactly only with `t < −T` or `t > T`, so a continuous path to the flexion branch must pass bends whose reach exceeds the target. The band does that over the first `d⁰ − R(T + W)` (≈ 8·(R_max − d⁰)) below the rest reach: Polybot arms 2.1 mm with the hand ≤ 0.57 mm past its target, Seed-san's 0.28 mm and ≤ 0.07 mm; the elbow moves ~3× faster than the ordinary straight-limb rate at bend T there (continuous, C¹ at the far end; the velocity reverses at the rest, where the hyperextension is largest). Rest exactness holds within 1e-7 on both fixtures, all four limbs. **Consequence of the first version** (a branch switch at `d⁰`): a 5.7 mm (Seed-san) / 12.4 mm (Polybot) elbow pop whenever an IK hand crossed the rest reach — when an arm was switched to IK at rest and the hand moved inward, or in playback. Residual: a near-straight, off-hinge FK elbow (`s < T`, the mid joint beside the reach ray) has two equally exact IK matches (hyperextended or flexed by `s`); matching takes the side of the FK mid joint (`m·h`), so it flips between them as `m·h` changes sign (β = 0 there, the pole does not move; the mid error reports it and import keeps such a limb FK). |
| M2A-A1-4 | **Rest pole** = `b⁰ + h⁰·(l1 + l2) − m⁰_off` with `m⁰_off` the part of the rest bend outside the hinge plane (`limb/records.ts`). | Equals §5.2.2's `b⁰ + p̂⁰·(l1 + l2)` for rests bent about the hinge or straight; keeps the rest twist exactly zero for off-hinge rests (pole in front of the knee, not beside it). |
| M2A-A1-5 | **Foot region of the mesh**: the rig measures the support of the foot node's whole subtree (toes and helper joints such as Seed-san's `heel.L`, which carries the heel), and the io reader counts a humanoid lower leg's vertices below its foot's rest position for the foot (contract comment of `SkeletonDesc.boneSupport` amended). | Polybot skins its heel 100 % to the shin, so the foot-dominant vertices all lie in front of the ankle and the heel fell back to the guess (8.2 cm instead of the mesh's 5.7 cm). Now both fixtures meet the 3 mm gates against three.js skinning. |
| M2A-A1-6 | **`matchIkToFk` matches the IK side only** (IK control and pole); `fk.toes` keeps its channels. With a ball roll the IK toes stay planted by the toes rule, so they differ from the FK toes by the ball roll (and the toes joint, above the ground ball pivot, moves by about its height × roll). Errors (`endErrorM`, `midErrorM`, `maxRotationErrorRad`) cover the three chain bones. | §5.6 lists only the IK control, twist and pole; keying `fk.toes` in a switch would change the FK pose at `f − 1` (seamless rule) and `matchRange` keys "the side controls and nothing else". |
| M2A-A1-7 | **Influencing curves skip ik2 limbs inactive over the clip** (`clipOpActivity`): a limb whose `@ikFk` has no keyed curve and a static value or default ≤ 0, or a stepped curve whose every key value is ≤ 0 and whose infinities cannot rise above them (no linear extrapolation; no cycleOffset that adds a rise per cycle: post with last > first, pre with last < first), adds nothing (its op writes nothing in that clip). Any other `@ikFk` curve counts conservatively. | §5.7: an M1 rig upgraded with `rig.addLimbIk` exports byte-identical files, and an imported FK fallback clip (one stepped `@ikFk` 0 key) keeps the M1 schedule. With the reads of M2A-A1-14 an inactive limb would only add its IK, pole and attribute curves (an FK clip has at most the `@ikFk` key); the rule keeps them out too. Headless gate: bake into the model, VRMA and animation GLB byte-identical. First version: cycleOffset infinities counted as inactive (fix round 1). |
| M2A-A1-8 | **Plant Feet on IK legs**: `plantFeetChannels(program, pose, pins, reference, channels?)` gives a leg whose `ikFk ≥ 0.5` IK control channels (`W_ik = Pin · K_end⁻¹ · F⁻¹`, `solveControlChannels`, eulerNear the reference); FK legs get the M1 solve. Attributes come from `channels`, else from the `'@name'` entries of `reference` (missing ones at their defaults). An IK pin out of the leg's reach warns like the FK path. | The operator (`pose.plantFeet`, A2) passes `currentValues` (TRS only today): **A2 passes the evaluated channel buffer, or merges `evaluation.attrValues` into the reference.** Until then a leg reads its default (legs 1), which is right unless a clip keys `@ikFk` or the roll. |
| M2A-A1-9 | **Import to IK details**: the IK keys sit at the union of the key times of the FK chain controls and their control ancestors (hips and cog motion moves the foot); their tangents are `step` when every contributing group is stepped, `auto` when any is auto or Hermite, else `linear` (IK keys are derived values, never Hermite); rotation channels that stay at rest are not written (M1 rule); `@twist` is not keyed. Rows and warnings follow `LIMB_IDS` order. Arms at their default ikFk 0 are not considered. | §5.8; deterministic reports. |
| M2A-A1-10 | **A second ik2 solver on the same limb** warns (`duplicate-binding`); the first in solver-id order owns the limb's state. A rest bent > 2° against the hinge warns `asymmetric-rest` with the amendment C band (`restBand` in `ik/core.ts`; Polybot: "L Arm: the rest pose bends the elbow 4.9° against its hinge (hyperextended); IK straightens it towards full reach and passes to the normal bend over the first 2.1 mm closer than the rest, where the hand may pass its target by up to 0.57 mm"; Seed-san's 1.4° stays below the threshold). | §5.2.1, §5.4 rule 4. |
| M2A-A1-11 | **Toes rule at `0 < w < 1`**: `C_w = K_end⁻¹ · Ball(w·ballRoll)⁻¹ · K_end`. | Equals §5.3 step 6's `slerp(I, C, w)` with the same pivot, without a slerp per frame. |
| M2A-A1-12 | **No allocation per evaluation**: per-frame numbers pass through buffers (a double argument or return value of a call the optimizer does not inline is boxed), the kernel's read-back of op-written worlds uses indexed loops, and `xfaFromMat4` uses `Math.sqrt` instead of `Math.hypot`. Checked by count: the young generation's used size grows by 0 bytes over thousands of evaluations (less the reading's own few kB, with no collection in between; a threshold of < 1 object per 1,000–1,600 evaluations leaves room for a one-off allocation of the optimizing compiler), on the synthetic rig at ikFk 1, blended and inside the amendment C band, and on Seed-san with four IK limbs; each check also sees one small object per evaluation (≥ 16 bytes). The earlier check (60,000 evaluations without a young-generation collection) stays. | §8 A1 DoD ("zero growth with `--expose-gc` heap sampling or a counting allocator shim"). `xfaFromMat4` also serves the inverse solvers, whose last bits may differ from M1 (Plant Feet, matching); exports do not use it. |
| M2A-A1-13 | **A spec gate was replaced** (§8 A1, import to IK): "the pole curves of IK limbs move by ≤ 0.05·L between consecutive frames" does not hold for a 24 fps walk with the pole L in front of the knee: on UAL `Walk_Loop` the pole travels 0.16·L per frame at mid-swing, smoothly (the leg swings ~9° per frame). The headless gate checks the intent instead: per frame the pole's direction from the knee turns no more than the thigh and shin do (+0.05 rad; measured −0.015 rad), and reports the travel. The synthetic unit walk (36 frames per cycle at 30 fps) keeps the literal 0.05·L. | A flip would turn the pole by ~180° in one frame. |
| M2A-A1-14 | **ik2 reads** the IK control, the pole and the upper bone's parent node only (§5.3 declarations). | The FK worlds of the chain are ordered by the `bone(n) → op` edges of the written nodes (§5.4 rule 2), so the FK chain controls are not needed for scheduling; declared as reads they put their curves into the influencing set of every track the op writes (§5.8: `fk.lowerLeg` and `fk.foot` keys counted for the thigh, a smooth `fk.foot` curve made a stepped IK thigh LINEAR and gave A5's keep set extra key times). First version declared them (fix round 1). |
